Homemade Prezel Bites
How-To-Make- Mini Pretzel Bites (Easy)

Steps

 Ingredients Alternatives
  1. Activate the yeast: Whisk warm water, yeast, and brown
    sugar in a large bowl. Let it sit for 5 minutes until frothy.
  2. Mix the dough: Add the melted butter, salt, and all-purpose flour to the yeast mixture.
    Stir until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Knead and rise: Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 4 to 5 minutes until smooth and elastic.
    Cover and let it rise for about 30 to 60 minutes until doubled in size
  4. Shape the bites: Punch down the dough and divide it into 6 to 8 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a long rope (about 10 to 24 inches long),
    then use a knife or bench scraper to slice them into 1-to-1.5-inch bite-sized pieces.
  5. The baking soda bath: Bring 3 quarts of water to a rolling boil in a large pot, then slowly and carefully stir in the baking soda. Drop the pretzel bites into the
    boiling water in small batches for 20 to 30 seconds. Use a slotted spoon to remove them and transfer them to a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  6. Salt and bake: Brush the tops with melted butter or an egg wash, and sprinkle generously with coarse salt (or leave plain for a sweet version). Bake at 425°F (218°C) for 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden brown.
  7. : Brush immediately with more melted butter upon taking them out of the oven.
  • Warm water (1 cup)
  • Instant or Active dry Yeast (2 1/2 teaspoons)
  • Brown Sugar (1 teaspoon)
  • Melted Butter (2 tablespoons)
  • All Purpose Flour (2 1/2 cups-3)
  • Salt (1 teaspoon)
  • Baking Soda (8-9 cups of water and 1/2 cup of baking soda)
  • Instead of Melted butter, you can use oil.
  • Instead of All Purpose Flour, you can use Bread Flour
