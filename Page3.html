Homemade Donut Bites:
How-To-Make Mini Donuts (easy)

Steps

 Ingredients Alternatives
  1. 1. Heat the oil: Pour 3 inches of vegetable oil into a heavy-bottomed pot and heat it to 350°F (175°C) using a candy thermometer.
  2. Mix dry ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, 3 tablespoons of sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cut in the butter: Grate the cold butter into the dry mixture and stir gently with a spoon until combined.
  4. Add the milk: Pour in the whole milk in two parts, stirring just until a soft, cohesive dough forms. Avoid overmixing.
  5. Shape the bites: Dust your hands with a little flour if the dough is sticky. Roll the dough into tight balls, about 1.5 tablespoons each.
  6. 6. Fry: Drop 2 to 3 dough balls into the hot oil at a time. Fry for about 90 seconds to 2 minutes, turning occasionally, until they turn golden brown.
  7. Coat and serve: Remove the bites with a slotted spoon or spider tool, drain them briefly on paper towels, and roll them in the cinnamon-sugar mix while still warm
  • All Purpose Flour (2 cups)
  • Granulated Sugar(3 tablespoons)
  • Salt(1 teaspoon)
  • Baking Powder(1 tablespoon)
  • Cold unsalted Butter (5-6 tablespoons)
  • Whole Milk(3/4 cup)
  • Vegetable oil( 3 inches deep for frying)
  • Cinnamon-Sugar Coating
  • Instead cold unsalyed butter, you can use cold salted butter.
    • Instead of Vegatable oil, you can use canola oil
  • Instead of Whole Milk, you can use Butter Milk
  • Instead of Granulated Sugar, you can use powdered sugar
