Homemade Mini Cupcakes:
How-To-Make Mini Cupcakes (easy)

Steps

 Ingredients Alternatives
  1. Prep the oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 24-cup mini muffin tin with mini paper liners.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients: In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Mix wet ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk the granulated sugar, melted butter, egg white, and vanilla extract until slightly thickened.
  4. Combine wet elements: Stir in the whole milk and sour cream until smooth
  5. Form the batter: Add the dry flour mixture into the wet mixture and whisk gently just until the batter is combined.
  6. Fill the tin: Spoon about 1 tablespoon of batter into each mini liner (fill them about two-thirds full).
  7. Bake: Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let them cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then move to a wire rack.
  8. Make the frosting: Beat the softened butter until creamy, gradually add the powdered sugar, vanilla, and heavy cream, and whip until light and fluffy. Pipe or spread onto the cooled mini cupcakes.
  • Cake Flour (3/4 cups)
  • Baking Powder (1tsp)
  • Salt (1/4 tsp)
  • Granulated Sugar (1/2 cups)
  • Melted Butter (1/4 cup)
  • Egg White (1 large)
  • Vanilla Extract (1 tsp)
  • Whole Milk (1/4 cups)
  • Sour Cream (1/4 cup)
  • Instead of Eggs, you can use aquafaba.
  • Instead of All Purpose Flour, you can use Gluten Free Flour
  • Instead of Whole Milk, you can use Almond Milk
  • Replace the Sour cream with plain dairy free yogurt
